# Google Play listing — draft

**App name (30):** Arcade Rush: 4 Arcade Games
**Short description (80):** Grow crowds, stack coins, dodge traffic & hold the line — 4 games, play offline!

**Full description:**
Four satisfying arcade games in one app — no internet needed!

🏃 CROWD RUSH — Swipe through math gates (+, −, ×, ÷) to grow a giant crowd, dodge
spinning bars and saws, smash the red crowd and take down the boss.

🛹 STACK SKATER — Ride a sky-high track above a foggy city, grab coin stacks and
guard your growing coin bar from spikes and tight squeezes. Climb the ×10 ramp!

🏎️ OVERTAKE — Weave an open-wheel racer through highway traffic. Near misses pay
out — upgrade Stamina, Power and Income and go further every run.

🌲 LAST BARRICADE — Hold a forest barricade against waves of shamblers. Grab SMGs,
shotguns and rifles. Endless mode after wave 10. Stylized and gore-free.

✔ One coin wallet for every game  ✔ Daily rewards  ✔ Plays offline
✔ Smooth on older phones (Low graphics mode)  ✔ Satisfying haptics & effects

**Category:** Arcade · **Tags:** casual, runner, racing, shooter, offline
**Content rating notes:** cartoon violence (non-graphic, no blood), ads.
